# Keep idle update scoring from taking triage dispatch turns

With update scoring enabled and no eligible packages, a cold serial worker
previously submitted scoring on alternate ticks. Scoring discovered the empty
queue after it had already taken the shared executor slot.

Before dispatch, the scheduler now checks the same package eligibility and
recovery predicates the scoring worker uses. Empty, paused-only and
inactive-only cold queues leave the turn available for triage. Eligible scoring
still alternates with triage, interrupted work can recover even when scoring is
disabled, and a warm scoring pass can still prune memo entries. The worker
opens its own session and rechecks work under the existing coordination lock.

The scheduler regression covers all five states plus disabled scoring and
warm memo cleanup. This change leaves shared provider rate and spend limits,
host policy and executor capacity in place. Independent scoring admission and
production throughput remain open.
