# Native benchmark for combined rate and spend controls (#1691)

The **Native capped AI benchmark** workflow now also compares serial and
four-slot triage under combined rate throttling and a daily cap on native
Ubuntu SQLite and MySQL. The two additional cross-server jobs use the same
eight investigations, four SSH follow-ups, synthetic delays and daily-cap
accounting as the existing cross-server fixture, with 1,000 starts per hour.
Each matrix job runs six matched pairs and alternates the capture order.

Look for the `native-capped-sqlite-ai-benchmark-paced` and
`native-capped-mysql-ai-benchmark-paced` artifacts. Each includes 12 raw
captures, the accepted series and provenance with the control mode and rate.
The pair comparator checks workload, pacing and budget equivalence. The
workflow also checks that each series reports the requested mode, rate and
host count. The original daily-cap-only jobs retain their artifact names.

Compare serial and parallel within each artifact. At 1,000 starts per hour,
the seven intervals between eight investigations impose at least 25.2 seconds
of pacing. Provider or evidence stages can take less time without materially
reducing end-to-end elapsed time. Treat these captures as synthetic fixture
measurements. Production throughput and spend acceptance remain open.
