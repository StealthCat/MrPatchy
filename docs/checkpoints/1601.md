# Summarize paired budget ledger timings (#1601)

## 2026-11-26

Roadmap A follow-up to #1304 and #1483. The repeated fixture comparison
now reports the median and range of within-pair p50 and p95 differences
for reservation and reconciliation. Each statistic includes the number
of pairs with validated measurements on both sides. Positive differences
mean the candidate took longer.

Run the existing `tools/compare_ai_benchmark_series.py` command with at
least three matched pairs. When measured, the results appear under
`budget_timing_differences`, keyed by `budget_reservation` and
`budget_reconciliation`. The one-pair comparator still checks timing
bounds and settled-call counts. Older and uncapped reports still compare
without a new section if no pair has two measured sides.

On v1.110.0 the mixed measured/older regression failed because the series
discarded ledger timing deltas. The updated test covers p50 and p95 and
excludes the one-sided pair. The adjacent test confirms no budget summary
appears when no pair supplies two measurements.

Replayed the SQLite and MySQL captures prepared for #1594. Apart from the
new budget section, both outputs matched the stored series exactly. Each
new statistic had three pairs. On those Windows fixtures, the median
paired reconciliation p95 difference was +0.876 ms on SQLite and +11.180 ms
on MySQL. The SQLite range extended to +999.594 ms. Those measurements do
not diagnose the cause or establish production performance.

This increment is reporting only. It does not change provider calls,
admission, ledger accounting, or benchmark workload. Production contention
and independent scoring admission remain open.
