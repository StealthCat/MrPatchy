Run `python -m pytest -q tests/test_ai_benchmark_native_records.py` to replay
the original six-pair series for each backend. Each capture completes eight
cross-server investigations with four follow-ups, 12 retained input stages
and 12 settled synthetic calls. The throttle admitted eight starts at
1,000 starts per hour, with peak provider concurrency of one in every capture.

Median within-pair elapsed differences (four slots minus one) were −0.001
seconds on SQLite and −0.0015 seconds on MySQL. Compared with the 25.2-second
pacing floor, the small differences show that this fixture spent most of its
time waiting for the next shared start. The comparison uses a loopback
provider, simulated SSH delay and synthetic pricing. Production throughput,
real provider spend and remote-database behavior remain open.
