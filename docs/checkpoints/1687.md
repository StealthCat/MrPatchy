# Optional update-scoring capacity (Roadmap A, #1687)

Set `MRPATCHY_AI_RESERVE_SCORING_SLOT=1` on workers eligible to become scheduler
coordinator and recreate those workers. The setting is available in the bundled
and worker-only Compose deployments and for native workers. It requires
`MRPATCHY_AI_CONCURRENCY` of at least 2. The default, `0`, keeps the shared
executor and its existing dispatch order.

The coordinator leaves one slot available for update scoring, which can admit a
batch on a subsequent scheduler tick even while its other AI slots are occupied.
The reserved slot may sit idle between batches. Workers without leadership can
fill their executor with triage. In-flight work counts toward the same capacity
through a leadership change and can finish during drain. Budget and provider
limits still govern actual calls.

The scheduler fixture uses pending triage futures and introduces a real scoring
candidate between scheduler ticks. It also exercises leadership gain and loss
and the final drain. Configuration tests cover invalid values and serial
concurrency. Production throughput and cross-worker acceptance remain open
under #1032.
