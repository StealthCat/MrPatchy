# Leave triage slots available when legacy update scoring is throttled

With a single AI executor slot, legacy update scoring could take alternate
turns even after reaching its hourly allowance. The dispatch check saw
candidate packages or a warm memo. Only the scoring worker checked the
allowance, after it had already taken a turn and acquired the scoring lock.

Dispatch now uses the same rolling-hour predicate as the scoring worker.
When the allowance is zero or exhausted, the slot is available for process
triage. Scoring becomes eligible again at the existing cutoff, and an
interrupted scoring run still gets a turn for reconciliation. The worker
rechecks under its existing lock. Modern admission modes, provider limits,
host policy and executor capacity keep their existing behavior.

The scheduler regression covers a zero allowance with pending packages, a
saturated allowance with a warm memo, the exact cutoff, and recovery with a
zero allowance. The first three cases failed on main at
`5a77984b4268fb29f3496779ffac103b17e2cdf9`. This is a Roadmap A scheduling
increment. Independent scoring capacity and production throughput
measurements remain open.
