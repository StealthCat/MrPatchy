## Request a commercial license

Contact Robert Cronin through the [official project's issues](https://github.com/StealthCat/MrPatchy/issues).
Describe the organization, deployment type, approximate managed endpoint count
and any MSP/hosting or redistribution requirements. Do not include secrets or
sensitive infrastructure information in a public issue.

Commercial scope, fees, term and any support obligations are agreed in writing.
There is no published price, automatic trial, checkout or license-key activation
in this release. Opening an inquiry does not authorize commercial use. License
terms are not enforced through new runtime lockouts or a payment service.
